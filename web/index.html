unified-fpkger

Dumps

Finds every sce_sys/param.sfo and param.json a few folders down and groups them by what they declare: each base game collects the updates and add-ons carrying its title ID, wherever they are and whatever they are named.

    Options marked "one dump only" are off while several dumps are selected.

    The console decides the platform; only the matching build options are used.

    After the build

    Only used for a PS4 remaster or separate build, which needs the base and patch mirrored apart instead of streamed.

    Irreversible. The console becomes the only other copy.
